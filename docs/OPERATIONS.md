# Operations

## Daily cashier operation

1. Start the POS client.
2. Search for the customer using NFC, employee code, TRN or name.
3. Add sale items to the basket.
4. Choose cash, cafeteria account or split tender.
5. Confirm balance, change, Available to Spend and Employee Amount Due.
6. Complete the transaction and allow the system to record the transaction snapshot.

## Supported counter workflows

- Employee cafeteria-account sale.
- Cash-customer sale.
- Split tender sale.
- Void current sale.
- Full refund.
- Partial refund.
- Multiple partial refunds.
- Customer payment against Employee Amount Due.

## Management operation

Management users can perform additional credit approval, stipend or card reduction, manual month opening, funding-policy maintenance, backup, restore and audit review from management tools. These operations are kept outside the cashier workflow.

## Reporting operation

Use the report center for daily close-out, weekly close-out, itemized purchases, employee summaries, department totals, period totals and dashboard review. Reports can be exported to spreadsheet, PDF, CSV and HTML formats.

## Support operation

IT should confirm service startup, health endpoint response, logs, local POS store access, service store access and FFP database connectivity separately. Publication failures should be investigated independently from cashier workflow failures.
