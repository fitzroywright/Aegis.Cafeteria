# Management Demo Narrative

## Audience

FFP management.

## Story

The demo shows a complete cafeteria operating day: employee eligibility, month opening, customer lookup, sale, split tender, refund, customer payment, management controls, reporting and audit visibility.

## Feature coverage

- Universal search using NFC, employee code, TRN and name.
- Employee and cash-customer sale workflows.
- Cash, cafeteria account and split tender payment handling.
- Void, refund and customer-payment controls.
- Monthly credit and company subsidy calculation.
- Additional credit and stipend or card reduction workflows.
- Dashboard and report exports.
- FFP integration and audit trail.

## Management message

The platform gives FFP a controlled cafeteria operation with faster cashier service, clearer employee balances, management approvals, auditable changes and usable reporting from one transaction record.
